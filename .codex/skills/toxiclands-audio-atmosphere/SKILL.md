# ToxicLands Audio & Atmosphere Skill

## Purpose

Preserve the sonic identity and atmospheric tension of ToxicLands.

Audio must reinforce:
- isolation;
- contamination;
- discomfort;
- survival;
- environmental horror;
- ecological decay.

The world should sound:
- wet;
- heavy;
- unstable;
- alive.

# Core Audio Philosophy

Silence is important.

ToxicLands should NOT:
- constantly play music;
- overwhelm the player with sound;
- feel cinematic all the time.

Instead:
- ambience drives tension;
- environmental sound drives immersion;
- silence creates discomfort.

---

# Core Sound Identity

The world should contain:

- rain;
- distant thunder;
- wind;
- breathing filters;
- mud footsteps;
- wet surfaces;
- metal creaks;
- root movement;
- distant machinery;
- organic pulses;
- toxic bubbling.

The environment itself should feel alive.

---

# Music Philosophy

Music should be:
- minimal;
- sparse;
- ambient;
- oppressive;
- restrained.

Music must support:
- exploration;
- dread;
- loneliness;
- uncertainty.

Avoid:
- heroic themes;
- orchestral excess;
- constant combat music;
- high-energy tracks.

---

# Atmosphere Layers

Each area should contain layered ambience.

Examples:

## Vila dos Filtrados
- weak generators;
- coughing;
- distant metal noise;
- dripping water;
- muffled voices;
- filter breathing.

## Toxic Fields
- wind;
- insects;
- unstable vegetation;
- toxic bubbling;
- distant metallic groans.

## Verdant Facilities
- malfunctioning systems;
- organic machinery;
- old announcements;
- electrical hum;
- breathing structures.

---

# Filter Audio Rules

Filters are central to identity.

Breathing and filtration sounds should:
- reinforce survival;
- create intimacy;
- reinforce vulnerability.

Avoid exaggerated sci-fi respirators.

---

# Creature Audio Rules

Creatures should sound:
- biological;
- painful;
- unstable;
- wet;
- partially mechanical.

Avoid:
- generic monster roars;
- fantasy creature sounds;
- cartoon aggression.

---

# Combat Audio Rules

Combat should sound:
- desperate;
- heavy;
- improvised;
- dangerous.

Avoid:
- flashy arcade feedback;
- exaggerated action-game audio;
- overly cinematic effects.

---

# UI Audio Rules

UI audio should:
- remain subtle;
- preserve immersion;
- avoid distraction.

Use:
- muted clicks;
- analog tones;
- degraded electronic sounds.

---

# Environmental Horror Rules

Horror should emerge from:
- ambience;
- distant sounds;
- environmental instability;
- biological movement;
- unnatural silence.

Avoid:
- constant jump scares;
- loud horror stingers;
- excessive shock audio.

---

# Performance Rules

Audio systems should remain lightweight.

Avoid:
- too many simultaneous loops;
- excessive layered effects;
- unnecessary processing.

Prioritize:
- stable playback;
- clean looping;
- efficient sound usage.

---

# Validation Checklist

Before approving audio work, verify:

- Does it reinforce atmosphere?
- Does it preserve isolation?
- Is silence used effectively?
- Does the world sound alive?
- Is the audio grounded?
- Does it avoid generic horror tropes?
- Is it lightweight enough for MVP?

---

# Response Format After Work

When finished, summarize:

1. audio systems or assets affected;
2. ambience goals;
3. atmosphere impact;
4. performance considerations;
5. immersion considerations;
6. risks or warnings;
7. what was intentionally avoided.
