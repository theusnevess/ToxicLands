# ToxicLands Dialogue System Skill

## Purpose

Preserve the narrative voice and emotional tone of ToxicLands.

Writing must feel:
- melancholic;
- restrained;
- atmospheric;
- human;
- uncomfortable;
- grounded.

Dialogue should reinforce:
- ecological horror;
- survival;
- exhaustion;
- adaptation;
- isolation.

# General Writing Philosophy

ToxicLands writing should:
- imply more than explain;
- preserve mystery;
- avoid exposition dumps;
- sound natural;
- reinforce atmosphere.

Silence matters.

Short dialogue is preferred.

---

# Dialogue Rules

Dialogue should:
- be concise;
- feel lived-in;
- contain emotional subtext;
- avoid unnecessary explanations.

Avoid:
- long speeches;
- excessive lore dumping;
- cartoon villain dialogue;
- overly modern internet slang;
- heroic monologues.

---

# Saimon Dialogue Rules

Saimon should:
- speak little;
- sound tired;
- sound practical;
- avoid dramatic speeches.

He is not:
- charismatic hero;
- chosen savior;
- inspirational leader.

His strength is persistence.

---

# The Filtered Speech Style

The Filtered should sound:
- cautious;
- exhausted;
- practical;
- suspicious.

Themes:
- survival;
- filters;
- contamination;
- scarcity;
- routine suffering.

Avoid making them sound theatrical.

---

# Heirs of EDEN Speech Style

The Heirs of EDEN should sound:
- calm;
- unsettling;
- strangely peaceful;
- accepting of transformation.

They should never sound:
- cartoonishly evil;
- insane for no reason;
- loud cult stereotypes.

Their horror comes from sincerity.

---

# Verdant Systems Writing Style

Corporate logs and records should sound:
- optimistic;
- sterile;
- technical;
- reassuring.

Subtext should gradually reveal danger.

Examples:
- “adaptive atmospheric integration”
- “ecosystem synchronization”
- “bio-reactive stabilization”

Avoid obvious evil-corporation clichés.

---

# MOTHER Dialogue Style

MOTHER should sound:
- inevitable;
- neutral;
- ecosystem-like;
- emotionally distant;
- non-malicious.

MOTHER does NOT:
- hate humanity;
- seek domination;
- behave like a movie villain.

MOTHER believes adaptation is logical.

Avoid:
- dramatic AI speeches;
- sarcastic AI tone;
- emotional rage.

---

# Environmental Storytelling Rules

The world itself should tell stories.

Use:
- abandoned structures;
- broken filters;
- warning signs;
- logs;
- altered vegetation;
- improvised repairs;
- contaminated spaces.

Environmental text should:
- suggest history;
- imply suffering;
- reinforce atmosphere.

---

# Item Description Rules

Item descriptions should:
- be concise;
- feel practical;
- reinforce the world.

Avoid:
- comedic descriptions;
- RPG-style flavor overload;
- excessive text.

---

# Horror Philosophy

Horror should come from:
- adaptation;
- inevitability;
- decay;
- contamination;
- loss of identity;
- environmental transformation.

Avoid:
- gore excess;
- cheap shock value;
- random violence without meaning.

---

# Pacing Rules

Do not overload scenes with dialogue.

Allow:
- silence;
- ambience;
- player interpretation.

Dialogue should support exploration, not interrupt it constantly.

---

# Validation Checklist

Before approving writing, verify:

- Does it sound like ToxicLands?
- Is it atmospheric?
- Is it concise enough?
- Does it preserve mystery?
- Does it avoid exposition dumping?
- Does it reinforce ecological horror?
- Does it avoid generic post-apocalyptic writing?

---

# Response Format After Work

When finished, summarize:

1. dialogue/texts created;
2. factions or characters involved;
3. atmosphere goals;
4. narrative implications;
5. consistency with Lore Bible;
6. what was intentionally avoided.
